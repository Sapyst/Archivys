# Prompts de ícones (Gemini)

## Bloco de estilo (colar antes de cada ícone)
```
Single game UI icon, pixel art, 32x32 sprite upscaled with no anti-aliasing, crisp hard pixels, limited palette (max 12 colors),
thick 1px black outline around the whole object, soft top-left light, dark fantasy medieval style, object centered and filling ~80%
of the canvas, front view, no text, no shadow on the ground. Solid flat pure magenta background (#FF00FF), nothing else.
```

## Recursos (HUD de cima)
- **Moedas:** `A small stack of three old gold coins with a worn embossed sun symbol, one coin slightly tilted, warm gold and amber tones.`
- **Fama:** `A five-pointed golden star with a small ribbon banner tied below it, bright gold with orange shading.`
- **Honra:** `A silver heater shield with a white laurel wreath engraved in the center, cool steel blue-grey tones.`
- **Relógio do jogo:** `A round brass pocket sundial / clock face with two dark hands and small notch marks, cream face, brass rim.`

## Recursos (HUD de baixo)
- **Provisões:** `A round rustic loaf of bread with two diagonal score marks and a small wedge of yellow cheese beside it, warm brown and golden tones.`
- **Bebida:** `A wooden tankard with iron bands, overflowing white foam on top, a handle on the right, brown wood and grey iron.`
- **Materiais:** `A single grey metal ingot bar stacked on a small rough chunk of ore, light reflection on the ingot, steel grey and dark slate tones.`
- **Favores (Rhosanim):** `A small rolled parchment tied with a thin white ribbon and closed with a pale ivory wax seal stamped with a sun and leaves, elegant elven gold accents.`

## Abas (barra de navegação)
- **Guilda:** `A small medieval guild house front with a pointed wooden roof, a hanging sign with a glowing blue orb, warm lit window, wood and stone.`
- **Heróis:** `A dented steel adventurer helmet with a short green feather plume, front view, steel grey with green accent.`
- **Quadro (contratos):** `A wooden notice board with two pinned parchment papers, one with a red wax seal, small iron nails, brown wood and cream paper.`
- **Bar:** `A small wooden barrel lying on its side with an iron tap and a drop of amber liquid, brown wood and grey iron bands.`
- **Obras (melhorias):** `A carpenter's hammer crossed with a hand saw, wooden handles and grey steel heads.`

## Extras úteis

- **Conforto:** `A small burning fireplace hearth seen from the front with a red rug in front, warm orange glow.`
- **Moral:** `A small smiling theater mask in warm gold, simple and readable.`
- **Fome:** `A small empty wooden bowl with a spoon, light brown.`
- **Descanso:** `A small bed with a blue blanket and a white pillow, with a tiny "z" shape made of pixels (no letters, abstract zigzag).`

## Dica
Gere cada ícone e depois peça: `Same icon, same style and colors, make 3 small variations.` — escolha a mais legível em tamanho pequeno.
